🌎 Visa Sponsorship

Supporting Global Talent for U.S. Careers

American Software Resources believes that great talent knows no borders. We provide services to U.S. Citizens as well as visa sponsorship and immigration assistance for qualified foreign candidates who meet U.S. employment eligibility criteria.


Programs Supported

  • H-1B Sponsorship (new filings & transfers)
  • OPT / CPT Employment
  • Green Card Sponsorship (EB-2 / EB-3)

Eligibility & Policy

  • Case-by-case sponsorship evaluation
  • Must meet education & experience requirements
  • Full USCIS & US DOL compliance
  • Transparent and ethical process

Typical Process

  • Application screening & evaluation
  • Strict eligibility verification
  • H-1B / LCA initiation (if applicable)
  • Filing via dedicated immigration partners
  • Smooth onboarding after status approval

Why Choose ASR

  • History of proven, successful visa filings
  • Dedicated legal coordination team
  • Clear communication at every single step
  • Long-term stability and project assignments
